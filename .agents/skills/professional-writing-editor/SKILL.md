# Professional writing
- Identify intended reader and register, mirror user examples when supplied.
- Preserve numbers, names, dates, roles, qualifications, citations and uncertainty. Never turn small experiments into statistically proven results.
- Remove AI defaults: inflated praise, marketing clichés, fake rhetorical contrasts, repetitive three-part structures, needless bold labels, monotonous rhythm, empty closings and overexplained background.
- Keep coherent substantial paragraphs where appropriate. Precision matters more than sounding informal.
- For explicit "humanize" tasks, consult the separately installed MIT-licensed humanizer skill. Do not load its long instructions into unrelated tasks.
- Compare output with source and flag any lost, distorted or newly invented fact before finalizing.
